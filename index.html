Applied AI

Operational Cognition Systems

AI systems in this portfolio are treated as decision-intelligence layers, not uncontrolled execution layers. They classify operational signals, reason over infrastructure state, surface risk, and route recommendations into human-governed lifecycle workflows.

Signal Layer

Logs, test failures, deployment events, and change metadata are normalized into one infrastructure context stream.

Reasoning Layer

Hybrid engine uses retrieval, known-failure patterns, and policy gates to build an operational hypothesis.

Recommendation Layer

Produces ranked fixes, blast-radius estimate, and rollback-safe recommendations with human approval before execution.

Measured by Impact

Success criteria: faster MTTR, fewer repeat incidents, and fewer risky production changes.
