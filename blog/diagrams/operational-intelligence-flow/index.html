Flow Details

Deployment Stage

Release events, canary analysis, rollout health. All deployment-related telemetry flows into the correlation engine.

Security Signals

Runtime policy violations, compliance drift, threat detections, and anomalous behavior patterns from production systems.

Correlation Engine

Ingests and correlates logs, metrics, events, traces into cohesive signal streams that AI can reason about.

AI Intelligence

LLM-powered analysis of correlated signals: RCA generation, anomaly ranking, remediation suggestions backed by evidence.

Human Layer

Ranked analysis, approval workflows, and decision records. Humans make the final call on remediation actions.

Feedback Loop

Outcomes feed back into the correlation engine to improve future analysis quality and reduce false positives.
